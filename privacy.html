PRIVACY POLICY

HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

Last Updated: August 20, 2024

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LeeOnTrack collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or apply to join our sim racing league. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled responsibly.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, Discord username (when provided voluntarily)
  • iRacing Information: iRacing name and membership number (required for league participation)
  • Application Details: Information provided in league applications
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information (collected automatically for website analytics)

How We Collect Information

We collect information through:

  • League application forms on our website
  • Email communications with us
  • Discord server participation
  • Automatic data collection when you visit our website

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process league applications and manage league participation
  • Communicate with you about league events, schedules, and updates
  • Maintain championship standings and race results
  • Improve our website and services
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • League Operations: Driver names, cars, and championship points are displayed publicly on our website standings table
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party services for website hosting and email delivery
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Storing data securely on protected servers
  • Using secure connections (HTTPS) for data transmission
  • Limiting access to personal information to authorised personnel only
  • Regular security reviews and updates

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses local storage to remember your preferences, such as cookie consent choices. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers. You can control your browser's cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services, including:

  • YouTube: For video content
  • TikTok: For social media content
  • Discord: For community communication
  • GitHub Pages: For website hosting

These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Social Media

When you interact with us on social media platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Discord, Facebook), the respective platform's privacy policy applies to your data on that platform.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to processing of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where consent has been given

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us:

  • Discord: LeeOnTrack
