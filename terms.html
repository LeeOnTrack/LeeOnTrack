League Participation

Participation in the LeeOnTrack league is subject to:

  • Successful completion of the application process
  • Acceptance of league rules and racing standards
  • Active iRacing membership in good standing
  • Commitment to fair racing and respectful conduct

League Applications

When applying to join the league, you must:

  • Provide accurate and complete information
  • Use your real iRacing name and membership number
  • Agree to abide by league rules and decisions
  • Maintain respectful communication with organisers and fellow drivers

We reserve the right to accept or reject applications at our discretion.

Member Conduct

All league members are expected to:

  • Race fairly and respect fellow drivers
  • Follow iRacing sporting regulations
  • Communicate respectfully in Discord and other channels
  • Accept steward decisions with good sportsmanship
  • Attend races punctually or notify organisers of absence

Racing Standards

Fair racing is fundamental to LeeOnTrack. Members must:

  • Leave racing room when overtaking or defending
  • Avoid dangerous or reckless driving
  • Follow track limits and regulations
  • Respect blue flags and give way to faster cars when appropriate
  • Use mirrors and be aware of surrounding cars

Fair Racing Guidelines

Specific racing standards include:

  • Overtaking: Overtakes should be clean and decisive. Do not force cars off track.
  • Defending: You may make one defensive move. Do not weave or block excessively.
  • Contact: Avoid contact whenever possible. Minor incidents may be reviewed; major incidents will be penalised.
  • Rejoining: When rejoining after an incident, do so safely and off the racing line.

League Administration

League organisers are responsible for:

  • Setting race schedules and regulations
  • Managing championship standings and points
  • Reviewing incidents and applying penalties where appropriate
  • Making decisions on league membership and participation
  • Communicating important information to members

Race Schedules

Race schedules are published on the website and may be subject to change. Organisers will notify members of significant schedule changes with reasonable notice where possible.

Schedule Changes

  • Add or remove races from the schedule

Members will be notified of schedule changes through Discord and website updates.

Penalties and Decisions

Incidents may be reviewed by league organisers. Penalties may include:

  • Time penalties added to race results
  • Points deductions
  • Grid penalties for future races
  • Warning or probation status
  • In severe cases, league membership suspension or removal

Organiser decisions are final. Members may appeal decisions by providing additional evidence, but must accept the final outcome.

Championship Standings

Championship standings are calculated based on:

  • Race finishing positions

Standings are published on the website and updated after each race round.
