Important Notice Posted: Wednesday 21st October 2026

🏁 LEEONTRACK – SUNDAY RACE NIGHT TIME CHANGE 🕡

With the UK clocks changing as British Summer Time ends, our Sunday night league will move to a 6:30pm UK start time, starting this Sunday.

This time will remain in place until the next UK clock change.

The revised start time is intended to better accommodate our international drivers, including those in the US and countries ahead of GMT, while helping those two or more hours ahead of the UK avoid a late finish.

📅 Please update your diaries and check what 6:30pm UK time means in your local time zone, as clock changes can happen on different dates around the world.

See you on track this Sunday at 6:30pm UK time! 🏎️🏁

Lee & Naven

LeeOnTrack League Organisers
